Usable depth of discharge
How much of the rated capacity the system may draw before it stops supplying the load. The rest is never touched, which protects the battery from deep discharge. The state-of-charge chart shows the usable part, so 0 % there means the reserve is spent, not that the cells are flat. LiFePO₄ 80–90 %, Li-ion about 80 %, lead-acid 50 %.
Round-trip efficiency
Extra loss for energy that goes through the battery instead of straight to the load: cell chemistry plus the charge and discharge electronics. Applied half on the way in and half on the way out. Do not include the inverter here if it is already in the performance ratio. Lithium cells 90–95 %, lead-acid about 80 %.
